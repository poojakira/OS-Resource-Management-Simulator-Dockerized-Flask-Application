RESOURCE CONTROL PLANE

Exclusive shared-resource leases with durable state.

A production-oriented evolution of a Fall 2024 OS resource-management lab. Coordinate GPU nodes, lab devices, staging environments, and other scarce assets through authenticated, expiring leases.

  • GET /readyz database readiness
